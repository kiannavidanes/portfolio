Pick Your Filter

For this project, I wanted to explore how you can alter one's perception of your identity based on how you present yourself online. I chose to alter the background images, hide the background of the flowers, and reveal the two paintings. I used digital processes such as masking, layering, and distortion through Photoshop. These processes affected this work's meaning because it shows two versions of the same thing (video game sheep vs. sacrificial lamb, Narcissus myth vs. narcissus flower), but blends them together. It functions specifically as an exploration of digital identity because the meaning of the work is directly tied to the process/creation of the image.
